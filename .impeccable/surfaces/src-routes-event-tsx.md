# /event — Cocoween

Scope: one public event page, mode Persuade. Audience: parents of young children (Spanish- and English-speaking, mostly on phones from Instagram) and Lighthill's existing clients. Job: understand what Cocoween is, what a child gets, when and where, then buy $60 tickets per child. Proof: the owners' real flyers, Fluffy Bee Ceramics partnership, Lighthill reviews. Constraints: kid-friendly (never scary or gory), respectful of Día de los Muertos, recognisably Lighthill, bilingual toggle on this page only, walk-in 1–4 PM with no time slots and no cap, Stripe price found by product metadata event=cocoween. Memorable moment: tapping a lotería card lights it from inside. Unresolved: none.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The event is a lotería tabla. Every thing a child gets is a numbered card, and buying is calling ¡Lotería!. It refuses the category default of a flyer-on-top page with an icon row and a Buy button.

OWN-WORLD: The ground is a night-violet table (#1c0f2e). The cards are printed card stock, each with a saturated field (marigold #f2a11f, rosa mexicano #e5007d, turquoise #00a29a, violeta #6b2fa3), a heavy black frame, a red wood-type number top-left and a wood-type title bottom. The tabla frame is a thick bone rule. The price is a round stamped seal. Light is the state: the selected card glows from inside like a candle, and state is also drawn by the frame (single, double, punched corner), never by colour alone. Papel-picado cut edges appear only on band transitions.

STORY: In the first viewport the visitor understands Cocoween as a Halloween and Día de los Muertos day for kids at Lighthill. They see three cards that say exactly what's included, and they believe it is real and well run (owners' flyer, partner, reviews). They set the number of children and press ¡Lotería!, which opens Stripe.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On desktop, the left 5/12 holds "LIGHTHILL STUDIO PRESENTS", a huge COCOWEEN title, a one-line subtitle and the EN/ES switch. The right 7/12 holds three upright cards fanned slightly on the table (La Cerámica, La Foto, Las Tres Fotos). Below them, across the full width, runs the tabla frame strip: Oct 31 · 1–4 PM · 1766 Old Norcross Rd · the $60 seal · a children − n + counter · the primary ¡Lotería! button. On phones the title comes first, then the cards in a 3-up horizontal deck, then the frame strip, sticky at the bottom.

FORM: Lotería tabla, grounded candidate 3 of 7 (ofrenda, papel picado, lotería, talavera, alebrijes, kermés tickets, trick-or-treat), seed key d76f4012. Raises: one card held at a time (detector), state by frame stroke (jackfield), strict card-height bands (film), light as state (akari), fixed tabla with in-place language swap (pc98), price in one stamped seal (googie).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
